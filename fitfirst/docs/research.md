# Desk research (Nov 2026) — not a substitute for real survey answers

Sources are mostly US/UK and about clothing shoppers in general, not specifically women who train.

| Finding | Source |
|---|---|
| Size/fit is the top reason for online apparel returns (53% of surveyed retailers); ~24% of online apparel orders are returned in the US | https://3dlook.ai/content-hub/the-true-cost-of-apparel-returns/ |
| 76% kept an item that doesn't fit to avoid the return process | https://newsroom.stitchfix.com/blog/new-research-from-stitch-fix-reveals-what-shoppers-struggle-with-most/ |
| 82% say their usual size always or often varies between brands | https://www.popsyclothing.co.uk/blogs/news/popsy-fit-fix-report-2026-online-clothing-sizing-uk |
| Counterpoint: activewear had among the fewest fit-related returns (7%) thanks to stretchy fabrics | same Popsy report |
| 44% of athletic women fell between sizes for at least one measurement across three legging brands | https://informahealthcare.com/doi/full/10.1080/17543266.2023.2275033 |
| 61% of UK shoppers hesitate to buy clothes online because of sizing | https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/?p=16225 |
| 60% don't believe sizing tech can be accurate | https://cxtoday.com/is-inadequate-online-sizing-technology-driving-customers-away |
| Pilates was the most-booked workout on ClassPass for the third year in 2025 | https://sgbonline.com/pilates-dominates-fitness-bookings-for-classpass-in-2025/ |
| Top women's athletic brands: Nike, Lululemon, Alo, Adidas; Gymshark strong with younger buyers | https://sgbonline.com/?p=336799 |
